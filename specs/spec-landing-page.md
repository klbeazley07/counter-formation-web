# Identity Pillar — Landing Page Spec

## Route & Entry Point

- **Route:** `/identity`
- **Entry point:** Architecture of the Soul → Identity pillar click-through
- **Narrative arc:** Confrontation → Theology → Revelation → Formation → Product → Action
- **Layout:** Long-scroll, single-column cinematic experience (different from the two-column individual piece pages)

The page follows the same liturgical cadence the brand guide describes: stillness, then scripture, then reflection, then action, stretched across a full scroll experience. The reader should feel like they are being walked into something, not sold something.

## Section A — Hero

**Purpose:** Stop the scroll. Name the problem. Set the tone.

**Headline:**
> YOU ARE BEING FORMED

**Subline (Cormorant Garamond italic, muted ivory):**
> Every satisfying explanation for your identity that doesn't start with God will eventually collapse under its own weight.

**Eyebrow Label (Champagne Gold #C9A84C, tracked wide):**

**Visual Direction:**
- Full-bleed Hero Black (`#06050A`) background
- The shield-white icon renders at large scale (40–50% viewport height) at reduced opacity (8–12%), positioned off-center as atmospheric texture, not focal point

- Subtle particle field (same system as homepage hero) with slow downward drift
- Shield watermark has barely perceptible parallax shift on scroll
- Bottom of hero: single downward chevron or thin gold line, animated to pulse gently
- Hero image: `/public/identity_wide.png`

## Section B — What Is the Armor of God?

**Purpose:** Teach. Ground the reader in Ephesians 6 without assuming they know the passage.

**Layout:** Centered reading column (`--cf-width-reading: 740px`). Text-forward, no sidebar.

**Eyebrow:** `EPHESIANS 6:10–18` in gold

**Scripture Block:** Full Ephesians 6:10–18 passage in Cormorant Garamond italic, larger size. Given room to breathe.

**Teaching Content (three ideas in flowing prose, no bullets):**

1. Paul is writing to people under real pressure, not offering a metaphor for self-improvement but a survival framework for people living inside a hostile formation system (Rome, emperor worship, cultural assimilation). Draw the parallel to modern formation pressure.

2. The armor is not something you build, it is something you receive and put on. Identity in Christ is given, not constructed. The armor represents character and truth that originate in God, not in your performance.

3. "Putting on" is a daily, deliberate act. You drift without it by default. Putting it on is a practice, which is why this collection pairs every piece with a formation pathway.

**Visual accent:** Thin horizontal gold rule separates scripture from teaching. Section ends with faded helmet watermark at 6–8% opacity.

## Section C — This Is God's Armor

**Purpose:** The theological revelation that changes how the reader sees the whole system.

**Layout:** Two-column at desktop (`--cf-width-content: 1100px`). Left: teaching content. Right: Isaiah 59:17 as large vertical typographic treatment.

**Core Insight:** The armor Paul describes is drawn from Isaiah's descriptions of God Himself. Isaiah 59:17 describes God putting on righteousness as a breastplate, salvation as a helmet. Isaiah 11:5 (belt of faithfulness), Isaiah 52:7 (feet bringing good news of peace). When you put on the armor of God, you are stepping into God's own character.

**Brand Line (Michroma, tracked wide, Champagne Gold, given its own vertical space):**
> "You are not inventing identity. You are receiving it."

**Background shift:** Subtly warms from Hero Black to Rule Brown (`#17140F`), gradient transition, not hard cut.

## Section D — The Six Pieces (Armor Breakdown)

**Purpose:** Formation backbone. Each piece gets theology, modern tension, daily practice, devotional hook, product tie-in.

**Layout:** Full-width sections (not card grid, not carousel). Alternate visual alignment (left-heavy, then right-heavy) for rhythm. Each links to its dedicated `/identity/[piece]` page.

### 01 · Belt of Truth
- **Scripture:** Ephesians 6:14a
- **Theology:** The belt was the first piece; everything else attached to it. Truth is foundational. Not abstract doctrine but lived reality.
- **Modern Tension:** Curated identity. Social media trains you to perform a self rather than know one.
- **Practice:** Five-minute evening examination rooted in Ignatian Examen.
- **Hook:** "What would change if you stopped managing your image and started telling the truth?"
- **Product:** Content-only at launch.

### 02 · Breastplate of Righteousness
- **Scripture:** Ephesians 6:14b
- **Theology:** Protects the heart. Positional righteousness, not moral performance. Christ's righteousness credited to you.
- **Modern Tension:** Performance engine. Worth = output.
- **Practice:** Morning declaration spoken aloud.
- **Hook:** "What metric are you using to determine your worth today?"
- **Product:** Content-only at launch.

### 03 · Gospel of Peace
- **Scripture:** Ephesians 6:15
- **Theology:** Roman sandals had cleats for standing firm. Peace is grounding, not absence of conflict.
- **Modern Tension:** Anxiety as ambient condition. Systems engineered for reactivity.
- **Practice:** "Peace pause" three times daily, sixty seconds of stillness.
- **Hook:** "What are you anxious about right now? What would it feel like to set it down?"
- **Product:** Content-only at launch.

### 04 · Shield of Faith
- **Scripture:** Ephesians 6:16
- **Theology:** Full-body *thureos* soaked in water to quench fire arrows. Faith is positioning, not feeling.
- **Modern Tension:** Flaming arrows are lies about identity, God's character, whether obedience is worth it.
- **Practice:** "Arrow log" to catch lies and answer with Scripture.
- **Hook:** "What lie keeps recurring? What would it take to simply refuse it?"
- **Product:** Hero SKU — Drop 002. Premium Everyday Tee with shield graphic.

### 05 · Helmet of Salvation
- **Scripture:** Ephesians 6:17a
- **Theology:** Protects the mind. Salvation as present reality and settled identity, not just future promise.
- **Modern Tension:** Mind is most contested territory. Anxiety, doom-scrolling, information overload.
- **Practice:** "Helmet check" — morning identity declaration before digital input.
- **Hook:** "What is the first thing your mind reaches for in the morning?"
- **Product:** Hero SKU — Drop 002. Technical Hoodie with helmet mark.

### 06 · Sword of the Spirit
- **Scripture:** Ephesians 6:17b
- **Theology:** Only offensive weapon. Scripture as living, active, spoken weapon. *Rhema* = specific utterance.
- **Modern Tension:** Biblical illiteracy at historic highs.
- **Practice:** Scripture memorization, one verse per week.
- **Hook:** "Could you answer with Scripture — not the gist of it, but the words themselves?"
- **Product:** Hero SKU — Drop 002. Technical Tee with sword mark. Tagline: "The Word is a weapon."

## Section E — Brand Integration

**Purpose:** Answer "why does a formation brand use armor imagery?"

**Layout:** Centered reading column. Prose-forward.

**Three connected ideas:**
1. The armor is not a costume. It is what God has provided for people in a hostile formation system.
2. The gear is not the armor. It is a marker, a daily reminder. The QR code connects to the formation content.
3. The gear is the entry point. The content is the formation. The practice is the armor.

**Closing line (Michroma, gold):**
> "The gear is not the mission. It's a marker of it."
