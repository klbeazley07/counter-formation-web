# Product System — Armor of God Collection (Drop 002)

## Design Philosophy

Drop 001 (Rule of Life) used rhythm and practice: Barlow Condensed for bold display, Cormorant Garamond for contemplative text, the "before" signature, sleeve scripture references. Visual language of dailiness.

Drop 002 shifts to identity and readiness. More declarative. Still restrained and minimal, but with weight and firmness.

**Unifying signature:** "Armor Up." in Cormorant Garamond italic, Champagne Gold ink, small understated scale. Appears on every piece.

**Ink system:** Two-color. Primary: ivory/bone on dark garments. Accent: Champagne Gold (scripture reference, "Armor Up." signature, QR code frame only).
