# Visual Identity — Armor of God Extension

## Color Palette — Identity Pillar Adjustments

No new brand colors. The Armor collection operates within the existing system.

**What shifts is color weighting:**
- Identity pillar pages lean harder into **Hero Black (`#06050A`)** as dominant background
- Rule Brown (`#17140F`) used only for transitional moments (Section C on landing page)
- **Gold usage intensifies:** each armor piece icon renders in Champagne Gold (`#C9A84C`) rather than ivory
- **Per-piece accent colors intentionally avoided.** All six pieces share the same palette. Distinction comes from typography, iconography, and content.

## Typography — Identity Pillar

**Michroma takes a more prominent role.** Armor piece names always in Michroma, all caps, tracked 0.12–0.16em.

**Cormorant Garamond** carries devotional content, scripture, pull quotes, prayers, and "Armor Up." signature.

**Inter** remains body workhorse for teaching, practice, UI, metadata.

**New element — armor piece numeral:** Each piece numbered 01–06. Large-scale Michroma at display size (120–180px desktop), muted to 8–10% opacity, positioned as background watermark behind hero content.

## Iconography — The Armor Icon System

Six individual icons functioning as a cohesive system. Same family as existing helmet and shield marks.

**Design principles:**
- Line weight consistency
- Geometric abstraction (not illustrative, not literal, not fantasy)
- Reducible to recognizable shape at 24px

**The six icons:**
1. **Belt of Truth** — horizontal band with single clasp at center
2. **Breastplate of Righteousness** — minimal chest plate, broad shoulders tapering to waist
3. **Gospel of Peace (Shoes)** — single footprint/sole mark, grounded and planted
4. **Shield of Faith** — shield shape alone, tall rectangular with slight curve (separate from full brand shield)
5. **Helmet of Salvation** — existing helmet mark, unchanged
6. **Sword of the Spirit** — vertical line with minimal crossguard

**System behavior:**
- Displayed together: horizontal row or 2×3 grid
- Order: Belt → Breastplate → Shoes → Shield → Helmet → Sword (Ephesians 6:14–17)
- Active/completed: Champagne Gold
- Inactive/upcoming: ivory at 25% opacity

## Layout System — Identity Pillar Pages

Individual pages (`/identity/[piece]`) follow **Rule of Life two-column desktop layout:**
- Prose content left
- Sticky sidebar with interactive widget right
- Hero image (16:9 `_wide` variant) at top
- Gold eyebrow with armor piece name and number
- Anchor scripture in Cormorant Garamond
- Theology content in flowing prose
- Practice steps
- Reflection questions
- Interactive widget in sidebar
- Formation Shareable ("Declare") component below devotional
- Further reading grid (4-up, linking to related Rule of Life rhythms and other armor pieces)
- Bottom navigation: previous/next armor piece

**Key distinction from Rule of Life:** Large-scale background numeral (01–06) in hero, armor piece icon in Champagne Gold replaces rhythm icon.

**Landing page** (`/identity`) is long-scroll single-column (see spec-landing-page.md).

## Photography — Identity Pillar

Hero images shift toward more gravitas and structure:
- Stone, metal, architectural textures
- Single light sources in darkness
- Solitary figures in postures of readiness (not rest)
- Desaturated or muted warm tones

**Avoid:** Fantasy armor, medieval reenactment, warrior/battle imagery. The armor is spiritual, not martial.

## "Armor Up." Campaign Mark

**Typographic spec:** Cormorant Garamond, italic, with period. Period signals completion and finality.

**Rendering:** Always Champagne Gold. Scale reads as signature, not headline.

**Placement:** Product garments, landing page CTA area, Formation Shareable completion cards, social media, email headers, packaging.

**Pairing rule:** Always near Ephesians 6 reference or helmet mark. Never floating alone.
